Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biriyani

Instructions

To make authentic Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani , start by marinating raw, bone-in chicken pieces in a rich mixture of yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, salt, lemon juice, biryani masala, fresh mint, coriander, green chilies, and crispy fried onions for at least two hours. Tip: Marinate the chicken for at least two hours to develop the best flavor. Next, boil washed and soaked long-grain basmati rice with whole aromatic spices until it is about 70% cooked, then drain the water immediately. Build your layers in a heavy-bottomed pot by spreading the marinated chicken at the bottom, topping it evenly with the hot rice, and finishing with fried onions, fresh herbs, ghee, and saffron-infused milk. Finally, seal the pot tightly with aluminum foil or dough to lock in the steam. Cooking tip: Keep the heat low while cooking under dum for 35 to 40 minutes. Let it rest for 10 minutes before gently fluffing the layers from the side.
